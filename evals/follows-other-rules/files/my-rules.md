# My betting rules

Strong home teams in the Premier League and La Liga, as singles or doubles.

## Ground rules
- Football only, Premier League and La Liga. Winner only, never a draw.

## Leagues
- Any home team in the top 6 of its league, playing a team in the bottom 6.
- Never an away team.

## When I drop a game
- The home team lost its last game.
