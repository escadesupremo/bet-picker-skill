# My betting rules

NFL moneylines against teams with a quarterback problem, as singles or 2-team parlays.

## Ground rules
- NFL only. Moneyline only, never spreads or totals.
- Bet against a team, never because I like the other one.

## Team list
| League | Team | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFL | Tennessee Titans | Fade | while the rookie starts |

## Leagues
- NFL: back the opponent of a team whose starting QB is out, or a backup or rookie is starting.
- Also back the opponent of a team in the Team list, whoever starts.

## When I drop a game
- The favourite is on a short week (played Thursday after a Sunday game).
- The game is in bad weather: heavy snow or strong wind.

## Internationals

## NFL
- Check the final injury report on the day of the game.

## Where to look
- Every NFL game in the date range.

## Clarifications
- "Starting QB is out": the player who started the team's last game isn't starting this one.
